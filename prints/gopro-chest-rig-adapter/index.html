Chest Rig to GoPro Hero4 Adapter

Designer · 2023 · 1 min read

3D-printed adapter that mounts a GoPro Hero4 securely to a MOLLE chest rig for hands-free footage

View on Thangs ↗

Overview

A camera mounting accessory that attaches a GoPro Hero4 directly to a chest rig, turning it into a stable, hands-free camera mount.

Problem

Standard GoPro mounts aren't built to interface with chest-rig webbing, making a stable, hands-free chest-mounted camera position awkward to achieve with off-the-shelf parts.

Constraints

  • Needs to interface with MOLLE chest-rig attachment points
  • Must hold the GoPro Hero4's standard mount securely during movement (hiking, biking, general activity)
  • Must be printable on a standard FDM printer

Approach

Designed an adapter bridging the GoPro Hero4's standard mount to MOLLE-compatible chest-rig attachment points, for a stable, secure hands-free mount.

Key Decisions

Target MOLLE compatibility directly

Reasoning:

Building to the MOLLE standard means the adapter works with chest rigs generally, not just one specific rig model.

Materials & Process

  • 3D CAD Modeling
  • FDM 3D Printing

Learnings
