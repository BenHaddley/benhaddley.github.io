Designing ToughTrack Around Barcode Workflows

workflow-designdata-integrity

Context

Verifying serialized equipment during a 502 check meant cross-referencing a paper list against physical gear by hand — slow, and one misread serial number away from an inventory record that doesn't match reality.

Decision

Build the entire verification flow around barcode scanning as the primary input, with the barcode field auto-focused and auto-submitting on a short pause, rather than a manual data-entry form.

Alternatives Considered

Manual serial number entry

Cons
  • Far slower for checking batches of gear
  • Error-prone — transposed digits on a long serial number are easy to miss

RFID tagging

Cons
  • Requires re-tagging existing equipment and dedicated reader hardware
  • Overkill for the actual problem — barcodes already exist on the gear

Reasoning

Scanning is fast enough that checking a whole group of laptops feels close to instant, and it removes transcription errors entirely since the scanner reads the exact printed serial. Keeping the barcode field always focused (re-grabbing focus every 100ms) means a physical barcode scanner — which just types characters fast and hits enter — works with zero clicking.
