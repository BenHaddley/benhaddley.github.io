How the investigation is taking shape

This is not a token-pump prediction exercise. It is a time-bounded reconstruction of what the bot could know at the instant it commits capital.

Phase 1 · Establish the behavior

Start with what the wallet actually does

The target buys only a subset of new launches and enters in the deployment slot. The first job is to quantify buy timing, in-block position, entry size, exits, holding time, and realized P&L before proposing any explanation.

deploy event → target buy → exit path → trade-level outcome
Phase 2 · Build the candidate universe

Turn chain activity into one row per decision

Every eligible deployment becomes a candidate. Tokens connect to deployers, funding wallets, transactions, metadata, trades, and eventual outcomes—but each field receives an explicit availability time.

Phase 3 · Enforce the cutoff

Keep only what existed at t_decision

Deployer history, wallet maturity, funding provenance, deployment construction, metadata, and timing are plausible inputs. Bonding-curve movement, later trades, and future deployer outcomes are not.

The non-negotiable rule: post-deployment data can measure whether a trade worked, but it cannot help decide whether to enter that trade.
Phase 4 · Infer a policy, not just a score

Test a small set of explainable hypotheses

The leading candidates are deployer reputation, wallet maturity, dev-buy intent, metadata quality, timing, and wallet-network relationships. An interpretable model can rank these signals while keeping the learned rule open to challenge.

Phase 5 · Replicate under friction

Make the backtest earn its conclusion

The score threshold is fixed on an earlier time window, then applied out of time. The replica is compared head-to-head with the target on overlap, P&L, hit rate, and drawdown, with fees, slippage, and one- to two-slot delays made visible.

train window → threshold lock → later deployments → delayed execution → comparison

Current principles

What will make the result credible

  • Every feature has an auditable timestamp and cutoff rule.
  • Model quality is measured out of time and with imbalance-aware metrics.
  • The final hypothesis is readable enough to challenge.
  • Execution delay, fees, slippage, and failed assumptions remain in the report.
